The Motivation: The Bandwidth vs. Latency Paradox

Problem Formulation

🌐 The Bandwidth Perspective

  • Text as Ultimate Compression: Transcribing acoustic speech into text drops bitrate to 100–150 bps (40–100× smaller than traditional G.711 / Opus codecs).
  • Shannon Capacity Limits: Mobile and satellite spectrum is crowded; compressing speech into compact symbolic tokens saves vital wireless channel uses.
  • The Common Premise: If S2T2S uses fewer bits, isn't it the obvious winner?

⏱️ The Time Efficiency Perspective

  • Modern Link Realities: Over 4G/5G/Wi-Fi, transmitting a 500-byte token payload takes < 3 ms. Physical transmission delay is negligible.
  • Human Interaction Limit: ITU-T G.114 mandates delay < 200 ms for natural interactive speech. Delays > 400 ms cause catastrophic conversational overlap.
  • The Question: What good is a 100 bps stream if the listener must wait 10 seconds before hearing the first word?

Theoretical Delay Formulation: Mathematical Roots of Latency

Mathematical Model
Total Delay = Tbuffering + Tencode + Ttransmission + Tdecode

1. The Context Bottleneck (Algorithmic)

  • ASR Sentence Lookahead: Models like Whisper / SenseVoice cannot emit grammatically correct text token-by-token in real time. They must buffer a complete clause:
    T_buffering ≈ Audio Duration (1.5s to 25s)
  • TTS Prosodic Context: Acoustic models require full sentences to compute natural pitch contours and cadence before generating the first PCM wave.

2. The Computational Cascade (Inference)

  • Sequential Generative Execution:
    1. SenseVoice ASR Forward Pass (250–1,200 ms)
    2. DeepSC Transformer Greedy Decoding (20–250 ms)
    3. Kokoro Neural TTS Synthesis (1,500–3,000 ms)
  • Semantic S2S Contrast: Operates on fixed 20 ms frames. Encoding takes ~5 ms, streaming vocoder takes ~15 ms. Total TTFA < 65 ms!

Empirical Result 1: Bandwidth Efficiency (S2T2S Wins)

Metric 1: Bitrate

Key Findings on Data Rate:

  • Short Utterances (<3s): S2T2S requires ~1,127 bps due to DeepSC start/end token symbols and 16-dim representation.
  • Extended Paragraphs (>20s): S2T2S effective bitrate drops to 622 bps!
  • Compression Superiority: S2T2S is up to 2.4× more bandwidth-efficient than SpeechTokenizer (1,500 bps) and 1.8× more efficient than Mimi (1,100 bps).
  • Linguistic Rationale: Human language syntax discards acoustic pitch, room acoustics, and breath sounds, transmitting pure conceptual semantics.

Empirical Result 2: Time-to-First-Audio (Semantic S2S Wins)

Metric 2: TTFA

Key Findings on Interactive Latency:

  • The 200 ms Conversational Standard: ITU-T G.114 mandates audio return under 200 ms for natural telepresence.
  • S2S Real-Time Mastery:
    • SpeechTokenizer: 63.0 ms
    • Kyutai Mimi: 43.0 ms
  • S2T2S Latency Explosion:
    • Short greeting: 5,278 ms

  • Empirical Speedup: Semantic S2S delivers the first audible speech wave 186× to 273× faster than cascade S2T2S!

Root Cause Analysis: Proving the Context Bottleneck

Latency Deconstruction

Why More Compute Cannot Fix S2T2S:

    🟥 Phrase Buffering: 7,248 ms (65.6% of total delay)
    🟧 SenseVoice Inference: 627 ms (5.7%)
    🟦 DeepSC Transmission: 175 ms (1.6%)

  • The Mathematical Proof: Even if compute latency was reduced to zero (Tcompute = 0), S2T2S would still require 7.25 seconds of delay due to linguistic phrase buffering.
  • Semantic S2S: Emits output after the very first 20 ms frame arrives!

Scaling Behavior: TTFA vs. Duration & Throughput (RTF)

Scaling Complexity

Duration Scaling & The RTF Nuance:

  • Algorithmic Complexity:
    • S2T2S: $\mathcal{O}(N)$ linear degradation. For every second the speaker talks, TTFA increases by 1 second.
    • S2S: $\mathcal{O}(1)$ constant latency. Fixed at 63 ms regardless of whether audio is 1 sec or 60 sec!
  • Real-Time Factor (RTF) Analysis:
    • S2T2S achieves an RTF of 0.06 to 0.15 on long texts (16× faster than real-time compute).
    • Crucial Lesson: Fast compute throughput is meaningless for conversational streaming if the pipeline is blocked behind an input phrase buffer.
