## ⚠️ Important Note
These are **synthetic formant-based speech approximations**, NOT real TTS output.
SenseVoice may or may not transcribe them correctly.

## Better option: Record real speech yourself!
Use your microphone directly in the UI for the most accurate test:
1. Run `run_ui.bat`
2. Click the microphone icon in the UI
3. Speak in Chinese (or any language)
4. Select the matching language from the dropdown

## Free Chinese audio samples (download manually)
If you want pre-recorded Chinese audio files, you can download them from:
- https://commonvoice.mozilla.org/zh-CN/datasets (Common Voice - free)
- https://freesound.org (search "Chinese speech")
- Or just use your phone to record yourself speaking Chinese!

## SenseVoice supported languages for best results
SenseVoice-Small natively supports: Chinese (zh), English (en), Japanese (ja), Korean (ko), Cantonese (yue)
For other languages (Hindi, French, Spanish etc.), select the language manually in the dropdown.
